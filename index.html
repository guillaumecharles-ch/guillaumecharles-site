The problem

Most organisations use about a third of what Nexthink can do.

Not for lack of capable teams. The platform gets deployed once, configured around the problems of that year, and then nobody has the time — or the outside perspective — to ask whether it is still pointed at the right things.

Dashboards get built and stop being opened. Modules that were bought never get switched on. Remote actions sit unused because nobody is quite sure what they will do. The telemetry keeps flowing, and the value quietly stops being claimed.

Then the renewal comes round, and someone has to answer the only question that really matters: what did we get for this?
